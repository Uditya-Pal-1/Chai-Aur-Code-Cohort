# Agentico — Competitive UI/UX Research

**Method.** The Mobbin MCP connected to this session refused every call ("requires a paid plan"), so the research was done on the live websites instead. I captured full-page screenshots and pulled the page structure (nav, H1–H3, CTAs) from 11 AI-agent companies, 5 of their pricing pages and 2 AI agencies. Contact sheets of each page are in `research/sheets/`.

## Patterns nearly every site uses

1. **Outcome-first H1, 2 lines, one emphasized word or phrase** (colored, italic serif or a rotating word): "Hire Lindy, *get it done*.", "[Email] agents that drive revenue", "Better outcomes. Built on Sierra."
2. **Dual CTA + microcopy under it**: "No credit card · Set up in 2 minutes" (Lindy), "14-day free trial" (Fin). Demo-led sites put an **inline work-email field** in the hero (Decagon, 11x, Beam).
3. **The product shown working, above the fold**: a real chat transcript or task feed, not an abstract illustration (Lindy Slack thread, Fin chat, Beam task table, Decagon AOP card).
4. **Logo wall right after the hero**, often grid-lined (11x, Relevance) or a scrolling marquee (CrewAI).
5. **3–4 step "how it works" process**: Build → Optimize → Scale (Decagon), Set up → Configure → Optimize (Fin), Week 1–2 / 3 / 4+ timeline (Relevance), Train → Run → Trace → Evaluate loop (CrewAI).
6. **Tabbed use cases by team or channel**: departments (Relevance, 11x), tasks (Lindy), channels Voice / Email / Chat (11x, Decagon).
7. **Big-number metric band plus metric-led case-study cards** (Decagon's colored brand cards "70% resolution", CrewAI "450M+", 11x "$4m pipeline").
8. **Trust/security block**: SOC 2 / GDPR / HIPAA badges + 3 promises (Lindy: Compliant · Private · In your control; Beam data residency map).
9. **Integrations grid** ("Connect to 1,000+ apps").
10. **FAQ accordion + full-width gradient final CTA + mega footer** (4–6 link columns).
11. **Announcement bar** at the very top (Fin, Decagon, CrewAI, 11x, Artisan).

## Pricing / paywall patterns

- **3 tiers, middle one highlighted** with a "Most popular" badge and a filled button; enterprise is "Custom" + "Talk to sales" (Lindy, Artisan, CrewAI).
- **A segmented toggle that changes the offer**: Startups vs Businesses (Artisan), with vs without Intercom (Fin).
- **Outcome-based pricing** is the category's main differentiator: Fin $0.99 per resolution, Sierra "pay only for a job well done".
- **Pricing explainers**: Lindy's "What's a credit?" cards, Fin's **ROI calculator**, CrewAI's long **feature comparison table**.
- **Free entry point that lowers risk**: "$50 in free credits" (Lindy), 14-day trial (Fin), free tier (CrewAI).
- **A conversational sales agent on the pricing page**: Artisan's "Aaron" side panel, CrewAI's "What would you like to learn about pricing?" box, Fin's "What would Fin cost for you?" input. The product sells itself.

## What the best sites do differently

- **They show the agent working, not features.** Lindy and Fin fill the hero with realistic conversations; weaker sites show abstract gradients (Morningside's long dark page with little content).
- **They talk in outcomes and time-to-value**: "first agent live in four weeks" (Beam), "go live in hours" (Fin), "ROI in weeks" (Relevance).
- **They frame autonomy as a ladder** (Relevance L1 Assisted → L4 Self-driving; Improving's maturity model). That calms buyers worried about handing control to AI.
- **They keep a human in the loop on screen**: Lindy's "Lindy preps the work → You approve it → Then it ships".
- **They make pricing tangible**: per-outcome prices, credit examples, ROI calculators instead of "Contact us".

## What this means for Agentico (an agency, not a platform)

- Lead with **business outcomes + a fixed-time promise** ("first agent live in 21 days").
- Hero: email capture + "Book a free AI audit", with a **live agent console** demo next to it.
- Sell **packages, not seats**: Pilot (fixed fee) · Growth (Most popular) · Enterprise (custom), with a **Build & hand-off vs Fully managed** toggle and an **outcome-based** add-on.
- Add an **ROI calculator**, a **comparison table**, an **autonomy ladder**, a **4-step engagement timeline**, **department tabs**, **metric case studies**, a **trust block**, an **FAQ**, and an **on-site AI concierge chat**. Agentico's own site should prove it can build agents.
- Style: warm off-white base (Lindy/Fin/Artisan) with one dark "engine room" section (Decagon/11x), a serif italic accent font (Fin/CrewAI/Artisan) over a clean sans, and a violet→coral gradient accent.
